# Vision

Build a premium digital experience that allows a local gym owner to manage members effortlessly while presenting the gym as a modern and trustworthy fitness brand.

The product should not feel like an admin panel.

It should feel like a polished SaaS product designed specifically for fitness businesses.

---

# Mission

Reduce manual work for gym owners by digitizing membership management while maintaining an extremely simple workflow.

The owner should immediately know:

- Who joined recently
- Who is active
- Who is expiring soon
- Who has expired
- Who needs a reminder

without opening multiple pages.

# Design Principles

Less is More

Every element must have a purpose.

Whitespace improves readability.

Motion should support usability, not distract.

Red should indicate importance, never decoration.

Glass surfaces should create depth, not reduce readability.

---

# Product Principles
